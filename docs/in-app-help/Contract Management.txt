 Contract Management

  What it is
  Contract Management gives you a dedicated place to store and organise supplier contracts, keeping them linked to the licenses they govern. Instead of hunting for agreements in a shared drive, you can find
  the contract and see exactly which licenses it covers from one view.

  How it works
  The Contracts page shows all contracts as a list, with a detail panel that opens when you select one. Within the detail panel you can view and edit the contract's fields, manage its folders and documents,
  and see which licenses are linked to it.

  Contracts support a folder structure. Within each contract you can create folders to organise the documents attached to it — for example, separating amendments from the original agreement. You can upload,
  download, and delete documents within those folders. Downloading a document triggers a file save directly in your browser.

  Contracts link to licenses primarily through the contract number shown on the license. When a license is created or its contract number is updated, LicenseTrack also looks up the matching contract record and stores a direct database reference (contract_id) where possible. The linked-licenses view in the contract detail panel currently matches licenses by contract number, case-insensitively, so keeping contract numbers consistent remains important. The contract_number field on the license is kept as the visible display and portability field.

  Deleting a folder is only allowed when the folder is empty. Remove or move its documents first, then delete the folder. Deleting a contract follows a cascade: all of its folders and their documents are permanently removed. There is no
  partial recovery — deletions are final.

  Key concepts
  Contract folder — a named grouping inside a contract for organising attached documents.
  Contract document — a file (such as a signed agreement or amendment) stored within a contract folder.
  Linked license — a license whose contract number matches the contract record. LicenseTrack also stores contract_id when it can resolve the contract number, but the visible linked-license list is based on the contract number.

  Who can use it
  Admins and Editors can create, edit, delete, and manage folders and documents. Viewers can read contracts and download documents, but only when at least one license linked to that contract falls within their
   assigned departments.

  Things to know
  - Deleting a contract is permanent and removes all its folders and documents in one action. There is no confirmation step beyond the initial prompt, so review the contract before deleting.
  - Deleting a contract folder does not cascade its documents. Non-empty folder deletion is rejected until the documents in that folder are removed.
  - A viewer whose departments do not overlap with any license linked to a given contract will not see that contract at all — the record is hidden entirely, not just partially restricted.
  - The contract_id link is resolved when a license is created or its contract number is updated. Licenses imported before this feature was introduced are backfilled automatically on the first startup after the migration. If a contract record for a given contract number does not exist yet, contract_id will be null until the contract is created and the license is updated.
  - Renaming a contract's contract number cascades the new number to all licenses with the old matching contract number, so those licenses remain visible from the contract's linked-license view.
