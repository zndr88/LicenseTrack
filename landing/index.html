  1. 01

    Organise your Registry.

Built for the operational record

The context behind every license.

Use LicenseTrack as your central registry or alongside discovery and compliance tools. It records procurement and entitlement evidence; it does not scan installations or reconcile compliance.

Check the product's fit →
Registry
Search, column grouping, saved views, PO line numbers, custom fields, completeness checks, and CSV import and export.
Evidence
Quotes, purchase orders, invoices, entitlements, and contracts linked to the right records.
Reports
Spend, publisher and supplier views, renewal calendars, and PDF exports.
Administration
Roles, audit history, database backups, optional SSO and email alerts, and API integrations.
