Built for the operational record

The context behind every license.

Use LicenseTrack as your central registry or alongside discovery and compliance tools. It records procurement and entitlement evidence; it does not scan installations or reconcile compliance.

Registry
Search, saved views, custom fields, completeness checks, and CSV import and export.
Evidence

Roles, audit history, database backups, optional SSO and email alerts, and API integrations.
