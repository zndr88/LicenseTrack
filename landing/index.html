A practical license-management workbench

LicenseTrack

LicenseTrack is a source-available, self-hosted tool for the part of software license management that too often lives in inboxes, shared drives, and spreadsheets: sourcing, purchase orders, license records, contracts, renewals, alerts, and reports. It was built from day-to-day license-management work.

Operational flow

For the gap between quote request and reliable license record.

01 Sourcing

Capture quote-stage work: supplier context, line items, estimated cost, renewal context, and quote documents.

02 Purchase orders

Move approved sourcing into pending orders, keep PO evidence with the order, and convert to an active license.

03 License Overview

Convert fulfilled orders into license records with procurement milestones, documents, and renewal-chain history.

Procurement pipeline

Keep the buying trail connected to the license.

The pipeline is built around real procurement handoffs: quote, PO, invoice, entitlement, and legacy cleanup. It does not replace an ERP or purchasing system. It gives the license owner a structured record that survives the handoff.

Sourcing Pending Active

Renewal workbench

Renewals start as work, not as a magic new record.

The workbench surfaces licenses entering the renewal window, highlights risk such as overdue dates, missing evidence, or high value, and starts the same sourcing-to-PO flow used for new purchases. The successor record is created only when the pending order is converted.

Scope

What LicenseTrack covers.

Overview

Searchable records, saved views, custom fields, completeness scoring, inline edits, CSV import and exports.

Records

Quotes, purchase orders, invoices, EULAs, entitlement files, contract folders, and document counts tied to the right scope.

Reports

Spend, portfolio shape, publisher and supplier views, renewal calendar, and easy to export to PDF.

Operations

Users, roles, OIDC, SMTP alerts, audit history, database backups, API tokens, webhooks, and integration hooks.
